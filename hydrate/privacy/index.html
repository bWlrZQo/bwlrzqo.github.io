Hydrate Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Effective November 1, 2026

Summary

Hydrate collects no data. The developer gets no data from the app. If you choose the text engine Claude, the app sends data to Anthropic with your own API key. Refer to the section Claude text engine.

Data on your iPhone

Your schedules, settings, and reminder texts stay on your iPhone. With the default text engine, message styles use the on-device model of Apple Intelligence. The texts are made on your iPhone and do not go to a server.

Claude text engine

This section applies only if you select the text engine Claude in Settings. The default text engine is on-device, and then no data goes out of your iPhone.

  • Before the app sends data, it shows a consent screen. The app sends nothing to Anthropic before you tap Allow.
  • The app sends the data with your own Anthropic API key. The developer does not see the key, the data, or the answers.
  • The data is the style prompts (the instructions and the tones), the example texts, and the texts of the style. The app also sends the earlier prompts and answers of the same run.
  • Anthropic gives the new texts back to the app. The privacy policy of Anthropic applies to this data.
  • Your API key stays in the Keychain of your iPhone. It is not part of a backup that moves to another device. You can remove the key in Settings.
  • Anthropic bills your account for the use. Settings shows the tokens and an estimated cost. The estimate can differ from the bill.

Notifications

Hydrate asks for permission to send notifications at the first launch. The reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules. Without this permission, the app does not schedule reminders.

Purchases

Apple handles the purchase of message styles. Hydrate gets only the result of the purchase from Apple. Hydrate does not get your payment details.

Bug reports

If you tap Report a Bug, your email app makes an email to the developer.

  • You see the email before you send it. Nothing is sent if you do not send it.
  • The email contains the app version and the iOS version.
  • The developer uses the email only to reply and to correct the bug.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page shows the new version number and its effective date.
