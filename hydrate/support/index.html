Report a bug

In the app, open About at the bottom of the main screen and tap Report a Bug. The email already contains the app version and the iOS version. Please add:

  • What you did, step by step.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened.
  • The time of a reminder that did not arrive, if the bug is about reminders.
  • A screenshot, if it helps.

Frequently asked questions

I do not get reminders

Make sure that notifications for Hydrate are on in the iOS Settings app. If they are off, the main screen shows “Notifications are off” and a button that opens Settings. Also make sure that the Reminders switch is on and that the day is not set to “Off”.

What does “Open Hydrate to keep reminders on” mean?

iOS lets an app schedule only a limited number of notifications. When you get this reminder, open Hydrate once, and the app schedules the next reminders.

Message styles are not available

Message styles use the on-device model of Apple Intelligence. They need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. If your iPhone supports it, turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings and wait until the model download is complete.

I bought message styles on another iPhone

Open the style picker and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that you used for the purchase.

Does Hydrate collect my data?

No. Refer to the privacy policy.
